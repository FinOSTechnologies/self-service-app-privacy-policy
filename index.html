1. About this policy

FinOS Technologies (“FinOS”, “we”, “us” or “our”) operates the FinOS Mobile app (“App”). This policy applies to the App for Android and iOS.

FinOS Mobile lets authorized customers of participating banks and financial institutions access banking services on their mobile devices. The information available to you depends on the services your financial institution enables and on your authorization.

The App connects to your financial institution’s Fineract/Mifos service. Records held by that service are managed by your financial institution and FinOS.

3. Information we handle

When you use the App, it handles information returned by or submitted to your financial institution’s Fineract/Mifos service, including:

  • your name, username, email, phone number, date of birth and gender;
  • client and office identifiers, your profile image, and address or city details where provided;
  • account identifiers, loan and savings information, balances, charges, repayments, transfers and transactions;
  • beneficiaries and guarantor information; and
  • notification messages.

The App also handles your login credentials and authentication tokens.

What stays on your device

Profile details are held in memory during your session and are not saved on your device. The App stores preferences and a notification registration token on your device. A local database stores charges and notifications and is cleared when you log out.

Camera and location

The App may use the camera to scan QR codes. The App does not access your device location.

4. How information is used

The App uses this information to:

  • authenticate you;
  • show and update your financial account information;
  • support loan and savings services;
  • process transfers and beneficiary details;
  • display notifications;
  • scan QR codes;
  • remember app preferences;
  • detect and prevent unauthorized activity and potential fraud;
  • maintain transaction records and send service notifications;
  • keep the App reliable and resolve technical issues; and
  • meet legal, regulatory and contractual obligations.

6. Storage and security

  • Your password is kept only in memory during Basic sign-in and is not saved on your device, so Basic sign-in users must sign in again after the app restarts.
  • If you sign in with OAuth, your sign-in token is stored in Android encrypted preferences or the iOS Keychain so you can stay signed in until the token expires, or you log out. Your app passcode is stored the same way.
  • Profile details are held in memory during your session and reloaded from the service after you sign in. User identifiers and profile images are not persisted in app settings.
  • App settings store preferences such as language, theme, service address, OAuth configuration, and a notification registration token. The local database has charge and notification tables; logout clears them.
  • Data is sent to the configured service and the identity provider over HTTPS.
  • Android HTTP request logging is enabled only in debug builds, with Authorization values redacted, and is disabled in release builds. The iOS app does not log HTTP requests.
  • On logout, the App clears user data, sign-in tokens, passcode, and local charge and notification rows.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Sharing and disclosure

  • Your financial institution’s service (Fineract/Mifos): the App sends and receives account, profile, transaction, beneficiary and notification information to provide the service. If you use a scanned QR code to add a beneficiary, the resulting beneficiary information is sent when you submit it.
  • Identity provider: when OAuth sign-in is used, sign-in requests are sent to the configured identity provider.
  • Notifications: the App sends a device notification registration token to the service so it can deliver notifications.
  • Maps: the Locations screen in the Android app shows a Google Map of our head office. Loading the map sends requests to Google, which can include technical information such as your IP address and device details. The App does not send your location or your account information to Google. See Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Barcode scanning: in the Android app, the App uses Google ML Kit to scan QR codes on your device. The App does not send scanned images to any server. Google’s ML Kit SDK may collect technical diagnostic and usage information, such as device model, operating system version, app name and version, and performance metrics, as described in Google’s ML Kit data disclosure. In the iOS app, QR codes are scanned on your device using AVFoundation; decoded beneficiary details are sent to the configured service only if you submit the beneficiary.
  • Service providers: providers that host, secure, maintain or support our services may process information for those purposes.
  • Legal and safety: we disclose information when required by law, legal process or a regulator, or when needed to protect people, rights or the security of our services.

The App does not include advertising SDKs or its own analytics tools. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

8. Retention and deletion

Logging out clears account data, sign-in tokens, passcode, and local charge and notification rows from your device.

Records held by the service are managed by your financial institution and FinOS. They are kept for the period required by the institution and applicable law, and as long as needed to provide the service, resolve disputes and keep the service secure. They are then deleted or de-identified unless the law requires continued retention.

To request access, correction or deletion, email support@finos.tech with enough information to verify your identity. Do not send passwords. Deletion requests are subject to legal retention duties, and requests about financial records may also need to go to your financial institution.

Some banking and transaction records may need to be kept even after you stop using the App or request account closure.

If the App offers account creation, FinOS and your financial institution will provide an account-deletion request process, subject to legal requirements and banking-record retention obligations.

9. Your rights and choices

You can manage device permissions, clear app data, and contact us about access, correction, deletion or other privacy requests at support@finos.tech. Requests concerning financial records may need to be handled by your financial institution.

Send privacy complaints and grievances to support@finos.tech or to FinOS Technologies at the address above. We aim to respond within one month.

10. Your responsibilities

You are responsible for keeping your login credentials and device secure and for using the App only for authorized purposes. Report suspected unauthorized access, suspicious transactions or security concerns to your financial institution promptly. Never share your password, PIN or OTP with anyone claiming to represent FinOS or your bank.

11. Children’s privacy

The App is not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly design the App to collect information from children under 18.
